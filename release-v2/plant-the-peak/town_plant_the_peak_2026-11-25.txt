Plant the Peak is a program designed to increase our overall tree canopy by providing professional installation of native trees on residential single family properties within Apex’s corporate limits, at no cost to the owner or tenant of the property.  The program covers 100% of the cost of one tree purchase and installation, per property. In 2026, the Plant the Peak program offered 234 trees to Apex residents. Apex Electric Department arborists will mark tree planting locations in November and December. Plantings will begin January 2027. Since 2021, the Plant the Peak program has purchased and installed over 500 trees around Apex! Plant the Peak 2026 Tree Care Guide 
 Plant the Peak Data Visualization Explore the interactive map to see the locations where Plant the Peak trees have been planted throughout the Town since 2021.  The map highlights planting sites and shows how the program is expanding the community’s tree canopy, one tree at a time.
