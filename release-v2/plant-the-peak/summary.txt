READ WITH CARE
  - The County's planting areas are places that were plantable in 2020 (lawn and open ground, after its exclusions), ranked for stormwater, heat and social equity. 'Outside' means the point is not on one of those polygons. It does not mean a low priority, and it does not mean the spot could not be planted.
  - A home built after 2020 stood on ground the County did not map as plantable, so points in newer neighbourhoods will mostly fall outside whatever their merit.
  - The strict count depends on where the Town put each point. With polygons this small, a point a few metres off its tree changes the answer; sections 2 and 3 bracket it.
  - Plant the Peak plants on request at residents' homes. This overlay describes where the trees went. It is not evidence about how sites were chosen.
