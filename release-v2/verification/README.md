# The check by eye: 375 before-and-after checks

The author looked at a before picture and an after picture for each clearing and gave a verdict: cleared, partly cleared, not cleared, or could not tell. Open a set to scroll through its pictures with the verdict under each one.

| Set | Checks | Scroll through it |
|---|---|---|
| The published count: all 238 clearings | 238 | [release/](release/README.md) |
| The 2017 to 2026 series: 100 clearings drawn at random | 100 | [sample/](sample/README.md) |
| The 2017 to 2026 series: the other 37 clearings over 10 acres | 37 | [large/](large/README.md) |

The two sets overlap on the ground: 70 of the 137 series checks are the same ground as a clearing in the published count. Four pictures are held back because the clearing sits mostly on one home lot ([PICTURES_HELD_BACK.csv](PICTURES_HELD_BACK.csv)); their verdicts are still listed.
